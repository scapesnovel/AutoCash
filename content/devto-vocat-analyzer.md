# Streamlining VoCat Modem Log Analysis

Analyzing modem logs shouldn't be a manual, error-prone task. I've built a zero-dependency, browser-based **VoCat Modem Log Analyzer** to help the VoCat community quickly identify errors in their logs.

### Why it matters
If you're using VoCat to manage modem infrastructure, you need immediate visibility into failures. This tool runs entirely in your browser—no data leaves your machine.
