# Visualizing Nested Agent Hierarchies: Free Online Tool

Agentic workflows are getting complex. As we move from single agents to nested hierarchies (subagents on subagents), tracking the stack depth becomes a major debugging challenge.

I've built the **Agent-Stack Depth Visualizer**—a zero-dependency, browser-based tool to help developers map and validate their agent hierarchies instantly.

### Why use it?
- **Instant Visualization:** Paste your agent-stack configuration and see the hierarchy levels.
- **Zero-Dependency:** Runs entirely in your browser. No data leaves your machine.
- **Validated Schemas:** Ensures your nested structures follow expected patterns.
